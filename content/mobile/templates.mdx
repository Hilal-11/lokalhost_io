Templates bundle the screens you need for a whole product — navigation, state and theming already wired — for both Expo and Flutter.

<TemplateGrid />

## What you get

- An Expo Router or Flutter `go_router` project with tabs, stacks and deep links configured.
- Every screen from the template, already connected to typed mock data you replace with your API.
- The shared theme, light and dark, plus app icon and splash placeholders.
